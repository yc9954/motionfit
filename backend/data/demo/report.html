8 Physics Modelling Assumptions

  • Rigid Links & Composite Inertia: Links are modelled as rigid hollow cylinders using density and wall thickness from materials.json, combined analytically with point-mass actuator housings at each joint origin.
  • Linear DC/BLDC Torque-Speed Envelope: Available actuator torque at joint velocity ω follows τ_avail(ω) = min(τ_stall_eff × max(0, 1 − |ω| / ω_nl_eff), τ_rated_eff).
  • Gearbox Scaling: External inline planetary stages scale stall and continuous torque by ratio × efficiency (capped at rated_output_torque_nm) and divide no-load speed by ratio.
  • Joint Damping & Payload: Joint viscous damping is fixed at 0.02 N·m·s/rad with 0.005 N·m dry friction loss. Payload (0.4 kg) is attached as a point mass at the end-effector tool flange.
  • 4-DOF Analytic Inverse Kinematics: Base yaw is solved via q1 = atan2(p_y, p_x) with planar 2-link elbow-up/down continuity across the 100 Hz trajectory.

9 Not Verified by Simulation (Mandatory Engineering Disclaimer)

  • Continuous Thermal Rise Under Ambient Conditions: Thermal safety is approximated by the RMS torque vs. continuous torque ratio (≤ 1.00); transient winding temperature in enclosed housings is not simulated.
  • Gearbox Backlash & Torsional Compliance: Spur and planetary gearhead lost motion (≤ 8–15 arcmin) and harmonic/shaft windup under reversing loads are not modelled.
  • Structural Deflection & Buckling: Link tubes are treated as rigid bodies; elastic bending and fastener shear must be checked in FEA.
  • Cable & Connector Routing: Harness mass, bend radius, and drag torque across J1–J4 are excluded.
  • Driver Current & Bus-Voltage Droop: Power supply sag under simultaneous multi-joint peak current draw is not simulated.
  • Environmental Collision & Contact Grasp Dynamics: Free-space trajectory tracking is verified; object slip, impact transients, and obstacle collisions are out of scope.
  • Mounting Hole Pattern Compatibility: Frame transitions (e.g., 28.5×46.5 mm XM430 vs. 33.5×58.5 mm XM540 or PG20 flange adapter) require mechanical bracket updates.

Engineering Note: Verify mechanical mounting flange geometry and continuous thermal dissipation under ambient enclosure conditions.
